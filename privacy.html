Privacy Policy — Senryaku Kigyo Uploader

Effective date: November 7, 2026

1. Operator

Senryaku Kigyo Uploader ("the Application") is developed and operated by an individual: the owner of the YouTube channel 戦略で読む企業史 (@senryaku-kigyo). Contact: mitunyansoncho@gmail.com

2. Who uses the Application

The Application is a personal tool used solely by the operator himself, on his own computer, with his own Google account. It is not offered to, distributed to, or used by any other person. There are no other users whose data the Application could access.

3. Data the Application accesses

The Application requests one Google OAuth scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. Through the YouTube Data API, the Application uploads video files, together with their titles, descriptions, custom thumbnails, and scheduled publication times, to the operator's own YouTube channel. The only Google user data involved is the operator's own YouTube channel and the content he himself publishes to it. The Application does not access Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or any other Google service, and it does not collect data about viewers or any third party.

4. How the data is used

The data described above is used for one purpose only: publishing the operator's own videos to his own YouTube channel. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any other purpose.

5. Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials and tokens for the operator's own account are stored only on the operator's personal computer. They are not transmitted to, stored on, or processed by any third-party server. Google user data is never sold, rented, or shared with any third party.

6. Retention and deletion

Stored tokens remain on the operator's computer until he revokes or deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions), which immediately prevents the Application from using the YouTube Data API. Locally stored tokens and credentials can be deleted simply by removing the files from the operator's computer. Questions about data handling can be sent to the contact email above.

7. Limited Use disclosure

The Application's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page together with a new effective date.
